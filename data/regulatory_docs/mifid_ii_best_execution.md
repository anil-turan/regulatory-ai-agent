# MiFID II — Best Execution and Conduct Requirements (Condensed Summary)

> **Source note**: Paraphrased summary of publicly known MiFID II (Directive
> 2014/65/EU) requirements, condensed for this demonstration project. Not a
> verbatim reproduction of the Directive or the FCA's onshored COBS rules —
> consult the official text before relying on this for real compliance work.

## Section 1 — Best Execution (Article 27 / COBS 11.2A)

### 1.1 Sufficient steps obligation
A firm must take all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result for
its clients, taking into account price, costs, speed, likelihood of execution
and settlement, size, nature, and any other relevant consideration.

### 1.2 Execution factors weighting
For retail clients, best execution is determined primarily by total
consideration, representing the price of the financial instrument and the
costs related to execution.

### 1.3 Order execution policy
A firm must establish and implement an order execution policy, provide
clients with appropriate information about it, and obtain prior client
consent to that policy.

### 1.4 Monitoring and review
A firm must monitor the effectiveness of its execution arrangements and
policy to identify and, where appropriate, correct deficiencies, and must
review the policy at least annually.

## Section 2 — Product Governance (Articles 16(3), 24(2))

### 2.1 Target market identification
Manufacturers must identify, at a sufficiently granular level, the target
market of end clients for each financial instrument and specify the type of
client for whose needs the product is compatible.

### 2.2 Distributor obligations
Distributors must understand the financial instruments they distribute,
assess the compatibility of those instruments with the needs of clients in
their target market, and ensure they are distributed to that target market.

## Section 3 — Inducements (Article 24(9))

### 3.1 General prohibition
A firm is not permitted to pay or receive fees, commissions or non-monetary
benefits in connection with investment services unless the payment or
benefit is designed to enhance the quality of the service and does not
impair compliance with the firm's duty to act in the client's best interest.

## Section 4 — Transaction Reporting (Article 26)

### 4.1 Reporting obligation
Investment firms which execute transactions in financial instruments must
report complete and accurate details of those transactions to the competent
authority as quickly as possible, and no later than the close of the
following working day.
