# FCA Handbook — SYSC (Senior Management Arrangements, Systems and Controls)

> **Source note**: This is a paraphrased, condensed summary of publicly known FCA SYSC
> requirements, written for demonstration purposes in this portfolio project. It is
> **not** a verbatim reproduction of the FCA Handbook and must not be relied on for
> real compliance decisions — always consult the current Handbook at
> handbook.fca.org.uk.

## SYSC 4 — General Organisational Requirements

### SYSC 4.1.1 — Robust governance arrangements
A firm must have robust governance arrangements, including a clear organisational
structure with well-defined, transparent and consistent lines of responsibility,
effective processes to identify, manage, monitor and report the risks it is or
might be exposed to, and adequate internal control mechanisms, including sound
administrative and accounting procedures.

### SYSC 4.1.8 — Business continuity
A firm must take reasonable steps to ensure continuity and regularity in the
performance of its regulated activities, employing appropriate and proportionate
systems, resources and procedures.

## SYSC 6 — Compliance, Internal Audit and Financial Crime

### SYSC 6.1.1 — Compliance function
A firm must establish, implement and maintain adequate policies and procedures
sufficient to ensure compliance of the firm, including its managers, employees
and appointed representatives, with its obligations under the regulatory system.

### SYSC 6.1.4 — Independence of compliance
A firm must ensure that a compliance function is established which operates
independently and has the necessary authority, resources, expertise and access
to all relevant information.

### SYSC 6.3.1 — Financial crime systems and controls
A firm must maintain policies and procedures to identify, assess, monitor and
manage money laundering risk, proportionate to the nature, scale and complexity
of its activities.

## SYSC 9 — Record Keeping

### SYSC 9.1.1
A firm must keep orderly records sufficient to enable the FCA to monitor
compliance with requirements under the regulatory system, particularly the
firm's compliance with all applicable obligations.

## SYSC 18 — Whistleblowing

### SYSC 18.3.1
A firm must establish, implement and maintain appropriate and effective
arrangements for disclosures of reportable concerns, including the ability
for workers to raise concerns confidentially and, if they wish, anonymously.

## PRIN — Principles for Businesses (cross-referenced by SYSC)

### PRIN 2.1 — Principle 3 (Management and control)
A firm must take reasonable care to organise and control its affairs
responsibly and effectively, with adequate risk management systems.

### PRIN 2.1 — Principle 11 (Relations with regulators)
A firm must deal with its regulators in an open and cooperative way, and must
disclose to the FCA anything relating to the firm of which the FCA would
reasonably expect notice.
