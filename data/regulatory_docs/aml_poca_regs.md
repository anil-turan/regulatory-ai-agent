# UK AML Framework — POCA 2002 & Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (Condensed Summary)

> **Source note**: Paraphrased summary of publicly known UK AML obligations under
> the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (POCA) and the Money Laundering, Terrorist
> Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017
> (MLR 2017), condensed for this demonstration project. Not a verbatim
> reproduction of the legislation — consult legislation.gov.uk before relying
> on this for real compliance work.

## Section 1 — Customer Due Diligence (MLR 2017, Regulation 27-28)

### 1.1 Standard due diligence
A firm must identify the customer and verify their identity on the basis of
documents, data or information obtained from a reliable and independent
source, before establishing a business relationship.

### 1.2 Enhanced due diligence triggers
Enhanced due diligence is required where the customer is a Politically
Exposed Person (PEP), where the business relationship or transaction
involves a high-risk third country, or in any other situation which by its
nature presents a higher risk of money laundering.

### 1.3 Ongoing monitoring
A firm must conduct ongoing monitoring of the business relationship,
including scrutiny of transactions to ensure they are consistent with the
firm's knowledge of the customer and their risk profile.

## Section 2 — Suspicious Activity Reporting (POCA 2002, Part 7)

### 2.1 Section 330 — Failure to disclose
A person in the regulated sector commits an offence if they know or suspect
(or have reasonable grounds to know or suspect) that another person is
engaged in money laundering, and fail to disclose this as soon as
practicable to the firm's nominated officer or the National Crime Agency
(NCA).

### 2.2 Section 331 — Nominated officer obligations
A nominated officer who receives a disclosure and knows or suspects money
laundering must disclose it to the NCA as soon as practicable via a
Suspicious Activity Report (SAR).

### 2.3 Section 333A — Tipping off
It is an offence to disclose that a SAR has been made, or that a money
laundering investigation is being contemplated or carried out, where that
disclosure is likely to prejudice the investigation.

## Section 3 — Internal Controls (MLR 2017, Regulation 19-21)

### 3.1 Risk assessment
A firm must carry out a written risk assessment identifying and assessing
the risks of money laundering and terrorist financing to which its business
is subject.

### 3.2 Policies, controls and procedures
A firm must establish and maintain policies, controls and procedures to
mitigate and manage the risks identified, proportionate to the size and
nature of the business.

### 3.3 Nominated officer (MLRO) appointment
A firm must appoint a nominated officer (commonly the Money Laundering
Reporting Officer, MLRO) responsible for receiving disclosures and deciding
whether to report to the NCA.

## Section 4 — Record Keeping (MLR 2017, Regulation 40)

### 4.1 Retention period
A firm must retain copies of documents and information obtained for
customer due diligence, and records of transactions, for five years
following the end of the business relationship.
